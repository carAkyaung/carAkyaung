What is carAkyaung?

carAkyaung is an automotive media project created for people who love Japanese cars, drift culture, tuning and motorsports. Our goal is to build a bridge between Japan and Myanmar by sharing knowledge, buying guides, maintenance tips and inspiring car stories.
